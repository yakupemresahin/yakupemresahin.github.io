I’m a doctoral researcher at the University of Liège in the Science with AI Lab (SAIL) led by Prof. Gilles Louppe, working on scientific machine learning.

I did my Master’s in Physics and AI at LMU Munich with a DAAD scholarship and wrote my thesis at Helmholtz AI in the group of Prof. Niki Kilbertus on symbolic regression for dynamical systems.

Before that, I completed a double major in Electrical & Electronics Engineering and Physics at Boğaziçi University, Istanbul, and did research internships at places including CERN and Koç University’s gravity group.

Research

Can machine learning learn the scientific models we don't have yet, rather than just emulating the ones we do or inferring their parameters?

That's the question behind my work. I build ML pipelines that learn scientific models directly from observations, combining generative models and Bayesian frameworks with latent world models, and probing those world models to see what physics they have actually learned. Right now I'm applying them to unfolding detector effects in particle physics. Weather and heliophysics are natural next targets.

If you find this question as mind-boggling as I do, feel free to reach out for a chat or collaboration!
